## 🎯 **PRESENTATION TIPS**

### **Before the Presentation:**
- **Test the demo** - Ensure platform is working perfectly
- **Prepare backup** - Have screenshots/videos ready
- **Know your audience** - Understand their priorities and concerns
- **Practice timing** - Rehearse to stay within time limits

### **During the Presentation:**
- **Start with the problem** - Make them feel the pain
- **Show, don't just tell** - Live demo is powerful
- **Use real examples** - South African municipalities and issues
- **Address concerns** - Be prepared for tough questions
- **End with clear next steps** - Make it easy to say yes

### **After the Presentation:**
- **Follow up quickly** - Send materials within 24 hours
- **Schedule follow-up** - Set specific next meeting
- **Provide additional info** - Answer any remaining questions
- **Maintain momentum** - Keep the conversation going
