# Citizen Image Evidence Policy

- Every citizen report requires ≥1 image (JPEG/PNG/WebP/HEIC, ≤10MB, ≤5 files).
- Original evidence is immutable; derived thumbnails/hashes stored separately.
- Content hash is server-authoritative.
- Images are not used to train external models without lawful authority.
- No facial recognition or demographic inference.
- Retention: originals follow municipal case retention; derived intelligence may be pruned after operational window (pilot: retain with case).
