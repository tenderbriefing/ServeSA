## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
We retain your information for specific periods based on purpose:

#### Service Delivery Data
- **Active Cases:** Retained until case resolution plus 7 years
- **Case History:** Retained for 7 years for audit and compliance
- **Communication Records:** Retained for 3 years
- **Location Data:** Retained for 2 years after last activity

#### Account Information
- **User Accounts:** Retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Retained for 2 years after last login
- **Account History:** Retained for 7 years after account closure
- **Authentication Logs:** Retained for 3 years

#### Analytics and Research
- **Usage Analytics:** Retained for 3 years
- **Performance Data:** Retained for 5 years
- **Research Data:** Retained for 7 years
- **Aggregated Data:** Retained indefinitely in anonymized form

### 6.2 Data Disposal
When retention periods expire:

#### Secure Disposal
- **Electronic Data:** Secure deletion and overwriting
- **Physical Records:** Secure shredding and destruction
- **Backup Data:** Secure deletion from backup systems
- **Third-Party Data:** Coordination with service providers

#### Disposal Verification
- **Audit Trails:** Documentation of disposal activities
- **Verification Procedures:** Confirmation of complete disposal
- **Certification:** Third-party verification when required
- **Record Keeping:** Retention of disposal records

## 14. Glossary

### 14.1 Key Terms
- **Personal Information:** Information relating to an identifiable individual
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal information
- **Data Controller:** Entity responsible for determining processing purposes
- **Data Processor:** Entity processing data on behalf of controller
- **Consent:** Voluntary, specific, and informed agreement to processing
- **Legitimate Interest:** Processing necessary for our legitimate interests
- **Data Breach:** Unauthorized access to or disclosure of personal information
- **Retention Period:** Time period for which data is kept
- **Data Portability:** Right to receive data in portable format
- **Automated Decision Making:** Decisions made without human intervention
