## 4. Service Delivery Reports

### 4.1 Report Submission
When submitting service delivery reports:
- **Accurate Information:** Provide accurate and complete information
- **Relevant Details:** Include sufficient detail for proper assessment
- **Appropriate Evidence:** Include relevant photos or documents when possible
- **Location Accuracy:** Provide accurate location information
- **Contact Information:** Provide valid contact information for updates

### 4.2 Report Processing
- **Assessment:** Reports will be assessed for validity and priority
- **Assignment:** Valid reports will be assigned to appropriate municipal departments
- **Updates:** You will receive regular updates on report progress
- **Resolution:** Reports will be resolved according to municipal procedures

### 4.3 Report Limitations
- **One Report per Issue:** Submit only one report per specific issue
- **No Duplicate Reports:** Do not submit duplicate reports for the same issue
- **Appropriate Categories:** Submit reports in appropriate categories
- **Valid Issues:** Only submit reports for legitimate service delivery issues
