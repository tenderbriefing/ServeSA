## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) establishes the framework for the pilot implementation of the ServeSA platform in [PILOT MUNICIPALITY NAME]. The pilot aims to test and validate the platform's effectiveness in improving service delivery reporting and resolution processes.

### 1.1 Objectives
- Test the ServeSA platform's functionality and user experience
- Validate georesolution accuracy for the municipality's wards
- Assess integration with existing municipal systems
- Evaluate SLA compliance and response times
- Gather feedback from municipal staff and residents
- Identify areas for improvement before full deployment

### 1.2 Scope
The pilot will cover:
- [X] wards within [PILOT MUNICIPALITY NAME]
- Service categories: Water, Electricity, Roads, Waste, Internet, Emergency
- Mobile-first web application access
- Integration with municipal case management systems
- Real-time notifications and status updates

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

### 4.1 Platform Availability
- **Target:** 99.9% uptime during pilot period
- **Monitoring:** Real-time uptime monitoring
- **Reporting:** Weekly availability reports

### 4.2 Response Times
- **Case Acknowledgment:** Within 2 hours of submission
- **Initial Response:** Within 24 hours for non-emergency cases
- **Emergency Cases:** Within 1 hour acknowledgment, 4 hours initial response
- **Resolution Updates:** Within 48 hours of status changes

### 4.3 Support Response
- **Critical Issues:** 2 hours during business hours
- **High Priority:** 4 hours during business hours
- **Medium Priority:** 24 hours
- **Low Priority:** 48 hours

## 5. Data Sharing and Privacy

### 5.1 Data Ownership
- Case data remains the property of [PILOT MUNICIPALITY NAME]
- Platform usage data belongs to ServeSA for improvement purposes
- Anonymized analytics may be shared for national reporting

### 5.2 Data Protection
- All data processing complies with POPIA
- Personal information is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Access controls limit data visibility to authorized personnel
- Data retention policies are clearly defined

### 5.3 Data Export
- Full case data export available at pilot conclusion
- Analytics and performance reports provided
- Integration data for municipal systems (if applicable)
